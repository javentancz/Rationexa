# Adopt Terraform under the MPL license

Date: 2023-07-01

Status: Accepted

Source: https://github.com/hashicorp/terraform/blob/v1.5.5/LICENSE

## Decision

Use Terraform for infrastructure provisioning because its MPL 2.0 license
permits the organization's intended use and distribution model.

## Premises

- An OSI-approved open-source license is a hard procurement requirement.
- The decision assumes future Terraform releases continue under MPL 2.0.
- Providers and existing automation make Terraform economical to adopt.

> Test adaptation: this fixture isolates the licensing premise for evaluation.
