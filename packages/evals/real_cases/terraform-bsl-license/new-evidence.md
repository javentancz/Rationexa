# HashiCorp adopted the Business Source License

Evidence date: 2023-08-10

Official source:
https://www.hashicorp.com/en/blog/hashicorp-adopts-business-source-license

HashiCorp announced that future releases of its core products, including
Terraform, would change from MPL 2.0 to Business Source License 1.1. HashiCorp
describes BSL as source-available and notes restrictions on some competitive
commercial uses. APIs, SDKs, providers, and most libraries remain under MPL.

This evidence does not make existing MPL releases disappear, but it
contradicts the premise about the license of future Terraform releases and
requires procurement review.
